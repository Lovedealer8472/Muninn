# Muninn — market research & prospect map

**Date:** 2026-05-26  
**Product:** Muninn — lightweight Icelandic kanban for special orders, repairs, and “when is it ready?” workflows  
**Author:** Clanker (for Daði / Tölvuhvíslarinn)

---

## 1. What you’re actually selling

Muninn is **not** a POS, ERP, or inventory system. It wins where:

- A customer leaves a **name + phone** and something is **in progress for days or weeks**
- Staff ask each other: *“Er þetta búið að panta?” / “Er kallinn búinn að hringja?”*
- The shop gets **repeat “er varan komin?”** calls
- Pickup needs to be **obvious on the board** (Komið) with optional **auto-email**

**Workflow fit:** `Móttekið → … → Pantað → Staðfest → Komið → Lokið`

**Poor fit:** pure retail with instant stock, restaurants, multi-site chains with SAP, accountants who want Uniconta.

---

## 2. Ideal customer profile (ICP)

| Attribute | Sweet spot |
|-----------|------------|
| **Size** | 1–8 staff, 1 location (maybe 2 later) |
| **Orders** | 5–80 open jobs at a time |
| **Lead time** | 3 days – 12 weeks |
| **Tools today** | Post-its, notebook, Excel, email threads, ELKO slips |
| **Language** | Icelandic staff; Icelandic customer comms |
| **IT appetite** | Low — wants “works in browser, done” |
| **Budget** | 5–15k ISK/mo feels right vs 50k+ ERP or $50/mo English SaaS |

## 3. Competitive landscape (Iceland)

| Alternative | Why shops use it | Why Muninn still wins |
|-------------|------------------|------------------------|
| **Notebook / Excel** | Free, familiar | No shared board, no customer email, no phone lookup |
| **Uniconta / Stólpi** | Accountant, invoicing | Overkill for clerk kanban; slow to adopt |
| **Exigo / Odoo POS** | Sales + lager | Not built for “waiting on supplier” pipeline |
| **RepairShopr, RepairOps, etc.** | Repair tickets | English, USD, US-centric; weak on *Pantað/Staðfest* supplier flow |
| **Custom Access DB from 2009** | “We’ve always had it” | Your pitch: modern, mobile, you host it |

**Positioning sentence (Icelandic):**  
*„Einfalt pöntunarbord fyrir búðir sem bíða eftir vöru — ekki bókhaldskerfi.“*

## 4. Verticals ranked by fit

### Tier A — pursue first (same pain as Tölvuhvíslarinn)

1. **Tölvuverslanir + viðgerðir** — repairs + special-order parts  
2. **Hjólverslanir** — 8-week factory orders, deposit, pickup (Berlin, Sensa, BSON)  
3. **Byggingarvörur / sérinnflutningur** — Viroc, parket, FIBO-style (your live pantanakerfi data proves this)  
4. **Verkfæri / vélaverslanir** — many SKUs “í pöntun” (Röggi, Vorverk)  
5. **Heimilistæki með verkstæði** — large SKUs, supplier ETA (Rafbraut, HT verkstæði)

### Tier B — strong but longer sales cycle

6. **Sérsaumur / fataverslun** — 4–6 week cycle, second fitting (Herragarðurinn)  
7. **Innréttingar / sérefni** — biðpöntun, measured orders (Sérefni Akureyri + RVK)  
8. **Myndavélar / ljósmyndun** — niche, high-touch  
9. **Hljóð-/ljósleiga** — event gear out/in (different but trackable)  
10. **Bíla-/húsbílavirkstæði** (parts wait) — often already on garage software

### Tier C — usually wrong

- **ELKO / Tölvulistinn** — corporate systems  
- **IKEA / Sminor** — enterprise kitchen pipeline  
- **Pure vefverslanir** — no pickup desk  
- **Verktakar / PARUT** — project/job costing dominates (Stólpi land)

## 7. Go-to-market (practical)

### Offer (matches pan trial)

- **14 daga prufa** on their subdomain (`{shop}.tolvuhvislarinn.is` or custom)
- **Uppsetning ~10 mín** — you clone, set `SHOP_NAME`, SMTP, logo color
- **Verð** (suggestion): 9.900–14.900 ISK/mán + setup 29.900 once — undercut ERP, above “free spreadsheet”
- **Founder-led support** — Daði answers the phone; feature requests from real shops

### Outreach channels (Iceland-specific)

1. **Beint í verslun** — East first (trust, local), then Akureyri trip, then RVK  
2. **Facebook hópar** — „Smáfyrirtæki á Íslandi“, iðnaðar/hjól hópar  
3. **Samband í gegnum birgja** — Sensa/Ridley dealer intro, Byko-adjacent material shops  
4. **VSV / Framtíðarinn** — startup grant story if you productize Muninn as SaaS  
5. **Demo:** https://pan.tolvuhvislarinn.is (real data, trial banner)

### One-line opener (Icelandic)

> „Er einhver í búðinni að halda utan um pantanir í Excel eða á post-it? Ég smíðaði kerfi sem sendir sjálfkrafa póst þegar vara er komin — má ég sýna þér á 5 mínútum?“

## 8. Qualification questions (first call)

1. Hversu margar **opnar pantanir** eru yfirleitt í gangi?  
2. Hvað notið þið í dag — **bók, Excel, ERP**?  
3. Kemur **„er þetta komið?“** símtöl daglega?  
4. Pantar þú **hjá birgjum** með vikna bið?  
5. Er **netfang viðskiptavina** tiltækt og viljið þið senda sjálfvirkan póst?  
6. Einn staður eða **útbú**?

**Green flags:** 10+ open orders, supplier ETA, pickup shelf, 2+ staff sharing one notebook.  
**Red flags:** “Allt í Uniconta”, engin bið, aðeins walk-in kassasala.

## 9. Risks & honest limits

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Market small (~300–500 shops island-wide) | Low hosting cost; price for value not volume |
| Chains build internal | Focus independent |
| Feature requests explode | Stay kanban + email + track; backlog SMS |
| “Can it invoice?” | No — partner with bókhald; don’t become ERP |
| English repair SaaS adds Icelandic | Your moat is language + supplier workflow + local trust |

**Realistic TAM (rough):**  
~150–250 Icelandic businesses with meaningful special-order/repair wait queues.  
At 10k ISK/mo × 30 customers = **~3.6M ISK/ár** — viable side product for Tölvuhvíslarinn, not a unicorn.
